GilongWorld — Sci-Fi Action RPG

GilongWorld is a homegrown Sci-Fi Action RPG developed in Vietnam by Long Gil Studio, pioneering the AAA Indie Game (Triple-I) movement. In GilongWorld, players master the art of alchemy and potion crafting, tame mysterious creatures to harness their unique abilities, construct and defend futuristic sci-fi bases, and survive in an expansive, dangerous world.
